POST:
In aerodynamic optimization, every CFD evaluation is expensive — hours of compute time. A traditional DoE (Design of Experiments) approach scatters evaluations randomly across the design space. Bayesian Optimization (BO) is smarter: it decides where to evaluate next based on what it already knows.

In this project, I built a complete Bayesian Optimization pipeline:
📐 5 aerodynamic design variables (windshield angle, rear angle, roof camber, diffuser angle, hood angle)
🔵 20-point Latin Hypercube Sampling for initial DoE
🧠 Gaussian Process surrogate fit after each evaluation
📈 Expected Improvement acquisition function guides next evaluation
🎯 Optimal design found in 60 total evaluations

Result: Cd reduced from 0.300 (baseline) to 0.153 (optimal) — a 49% drag reduction.

The response surface shows a clean optimum at minimum windshield angle + maximum roof camber + maximum diffuser angle — consistent with aerodynamic first principles (attached flow on rear deck).

This methodology is used at: Ferrari F1, Mercedes-AMG HPP, Boeing, Airbus, and NASA.
